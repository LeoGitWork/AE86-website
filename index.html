Toyota AE86 Trueno

The Toyota AE86 Trueno is a classic Japanese sports car produced from 1983 to 1987. It is known for its lightweight design, rear wheel drive layout, and pop up headlights. The AE86 became popular in the drifting community because of its handling and balance. It also gained worldwide recognition from the Japanese anime Initial D, where it became an iconic car among enthusiasts.
